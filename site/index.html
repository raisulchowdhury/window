A MENU BAR APP FOR THE DRIVE HOME

Leave work when the drive is shortest.

Window checks Apple Maps drive times across the half hour you could leave, then suggests when to go so you spend less time in traffic and still get home with time to spare.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 1Set it up once. Your office, your home, the half hour you could leave, and when you need to be home.
  2. 2Window checks the drive. It asks Apple Maps for drive-time estimates across that half hour and suggests a time.
  3. 3Pick a time and it watches. You get a nudge to wrap up, then one to leave.

Two nudges, then you go.

Time to wrap up17:20

While it watches, Window rechecks traffic every 3 minutes. If the plan stops working, it tells you and offers a new time.
